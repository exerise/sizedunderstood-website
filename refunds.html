Refund policy

Last updated: 12 November 2026

All sales are final.

The Pack is a digital download. Once you have it, you keep a copy forever, so we can't take it back and we don't offer refunds. Please check the requirements and ask us anything before you buy.

Why we don't offer refunds

Unlike a physical product, a downloaded file can't be returned. After download you have the full Pack for good, so a refund would mean giving the product away. That's why every sale is final, including for change of mind, not using the Pack, or buying by mistake.

Your right to cancel

Online purchases in the UK usually come with a 14-day right to cancel. For digital content, that right ends once the download starts, provided you agreed to immediate access and acknowledged losing the right. Before you pay, we ask you to tick a box confirming both, your agreement is recorded with your order, and we confirm it to you by email.

Before you buy

  • Check the requirements: Claude Code, operating system, hardware and a voice key.
  • Read what's included and the FAQ.
  • Unsure if it will work for you? Email support@sizedunderstood.com before buying and we'll tell you honestly.

If something doesn't work

Email support@sizedunderstood.com with your order number and what's going wrong. We'll help you get it running, usually within 24 hours, and ship fixes in free updates.

Your statutory rights

This policy doesn't affect your rights under the Consumer Rights Act 2015. Digital content must be as described, fit for purpose and of satisfactory quality. If the Pack is faulty, you're entitled to a repair or replacement, and if we can't fix it within a reasonable time, to some or all of your money back.

Duplicate or unauthorised payments

If you were charged twice for the same order, or a payment was made without your permission, email us and we'll put it right through Polar.

Please contact us before disputing a charge

If something's wrong, email us first. We'll respond quickly and sort it out with you directly.
