> run demo_

  1. Ask in plain English

    Type /explainer and any question your audience asks, like "what happens when an undersea internet cable gets cut".

  2. Research, every fact sourced

    Claude Code reads real sources first and saves every number it will say with its link, so nothing on screen is made up.

  3. Approve the script

    A tight 60–90 second script with 3 hooks to pick from, both spoken and visual, before anything is built. Choose yours and change any words. That's your only sign-off.

  4. Voice, one take

    The voiceover is recorded, paced and timed so every caption word lands as it's spoken.

  5. Every scene built in 3D

    Each sentence gets its own lit 3D scene, drawn from real references and checked frame by frame. Everyday objects start from the 10+ 3D models already in the folder, so builds go faster.

  6. Render with music and sound

    A music bed and sound effects are mixed under the voice. You get an MP4 in up to 4K at 60 fps, plus the post caption and the cover.

How do I get the files after I pay?

As soon as your payment goes through, Polar emails you a receipt with a link to your customer portal, where you download the pack. You can sign in to that portal with your email and download it again at any time.

Can I get a refund?

No. The pack is a digital download that you keep forever, so all sales are final. Please check the requirements before buying, and email support@sizedunderstood.com if you're unsure whether it will work for you.

If something doesn't work after you buy, we'll help you fix it. Your legal rights for faulty digital content aren't affected. See the refund policy.

Do I get updates?

Yes. Every future update is free. When a new version is out, we email you and your existing download link gives you the latest files. Updates keep the reels and assets you've already made.

Can I use the reels commercially?

Yes. The videos you make are yours: post them, monetise them, use them in ads or make them for clients. What you can't do is resell or share the pack files themselves. The terms and licence spell it out.

Can I share the pack with my team?

Each licence is for one person. If teammates will use the pack too, email business@sizedunderstood.com for multiple licences and team pricing.

Do you do sponsorships or affiliate partnerships?

Yes. For sponsored explainers on the SizedUnderstood channels, affiliate partnerships, multiple licences or anything else business-related, email business@sizedunderstood.com.

Do I need to know Remotion, Three.js or how to code?

No. Claude Code does the building. Knowing a little helps if you want to customise scenes by hand, but you can make full reels just by talking to Claude Code.

Are there other costs?

You need your own Claude Code access (a Claude Pro or Max plan, or an Anthropic API key) or an account with another supported agent. The AI voice needs a key from ElevenLabs, OpenAI or Fish Audio, billed by them; ElevenLabs has a free account, and you can use your own voice instead. Generated music and sound effects need an ElevenLabs plan that includes them, or you can use your own royalty-free files. Remotion, which renders the videos, is free for individuals and small companies; larger companies need a Remotion company licence.

Does it work with Codex, Cursor or other agents?

Yes. Setup also works with Codex, Cursor, Gemini and Grok, using your own account with them. The pack was built in Claude Code, so that's the agent shown on this page.
